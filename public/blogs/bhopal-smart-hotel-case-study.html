📍 Bhopal Case Study: Amardeep Hotel Ka Digital Transformation

Humne Bhopal Bus Stand aur Station area ke paas ke independent hotels ka survey kiya, jahan walk-in aur direct digital travelers sabse zyada aate hain. Amardeep Hotel ne jab hamara standalone system test kiya, to unhone counter par ek simple, premium black-and-gold QR code lagaya.

Guest ke aate hi unka Aadhaar card hamare AI ID Scanner se sirf 1 second me scan ho jata hai, jisse reception par queue lagna khatam ho gayi. Jaise hi check-in hua, guest ke WhatsApp par direct unka Digital Room Companion Kit chala gaya, jisme room number aur in-house Wi-Fi password pehle se compressed tha.

📈 Result Kya Nikla?

  • Zero OTA Commission: Pehle jo bookings OTA ke throuh aati thin, wo ab unke direct custom dynamic landing page se aane lagin, jisse har booking par ₹300-₹500 ka sidha munafa badha.
  • Staff Efficiency: GRC-compliant entry automation ke chalte night staff par se manual registration ka pressure bilkul khatam ho gaya.
